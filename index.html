Conceptual Site Overview

Business Goal

Create a polished, front-facing ecommerce website that helps Pet Supply Plus attract shoppers, promote products, and support customers with clear self-service tools.

Target Audience

Busy pet owners shopping on desktop, tablet, and smartphone who need easy navigation, product discovery, and quick access to service information.

UI Deliverables

Responsive layouts, consistent navigation, working internal links, shopping UI, checkout prototype, order status display, and support chat interface.
